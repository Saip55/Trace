MindCare AI - Privacy Statement

1. Data Sovereignty & Confidentiality: MindCare AI operates as a public-service mental health support system under the National Helpline Against Atrocities (14566). All clinical responses and assessments are protected under the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act.


2. Anonymous Reporting: Users may choose to submit assessments anonymously. No IP addresses or device trackers are linked to mental health evaluations.


3. Counselor Sharing: Your information is only accessible by accredited, certified trauma psychologists assigned to your case for therapeutic care and case correlation.


4. Security Protocols: All traffic is encrypted via TLS 1.3 and stored in compliant government cloud infrastructure.

Terms of Service

1. Nature of Service: MindCare AI provides AI-assisted psychological screening and emergency resource coordination. It is not an emergency hospital inpatient admission platform.


2. Emergency Situations: If you are experiencing imminent physical danger or life-threatening psychiatric distress, call 14566, 108, or 100 immediately.


3. Non-Commercial Guarantee: This service is entirely free of charge for all victims, complainants, and families.
